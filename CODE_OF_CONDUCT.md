# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Anchor to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their background, identity or experience level.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and treating other people's time as valuable.
- Accepting constructive feedback, and giving it about the work rather than the
  person.
- Assuming good faith when something reads badly.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
place where someone is representing the project.

Report problems to the maintainer through a private GitHub security advisory or
a direct message. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit,
hide or remove contributions, and may block accounts, when this document is not
respected.
